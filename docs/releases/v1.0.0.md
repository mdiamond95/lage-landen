Lage Landen is a Duolingo-style web app for the history and culture of the Netherlands and Belgium, built around maps, pictures and timelines. Version 1 completes the project plan: 26 units in four tracks, and the features around them.

Play it at https://mdiamond95.github.io/lage-landen/ (it works offline once loaded and installs on a phone's home screen).

## The 26 units

Each unit has six or seven lessons (five in Unit 1), a map story and a checkpoint: 156 lessons in all.

**Shared history, to 1830**

1. Land and water
2. Celts, Romans and Franks (57 BCE to 900)
3. Counts, bishops and cloth cities (900 to 1384)
4. The Burgundian Netherlands (1384 to 1555)
5. Revolt and the great split (1555 to 1648)
6. Golden Age and Spanish Netherlands (1588 to 1700)
7. Trade, empire and slavery (1600 to 1863)
8. Disaster year to Waterloo (1672 to 1815)
9. One kingdom, then two (1815 to 1839)

**The Netherlands, 1830 to today**

10. Constitution and pillars (1848 to 1914)
11. Occupation and liberation (1914 to 1949)
12. Water, welfare and tolerance (1932 to today)

**Belgium, 1830 to today**

13. Industry and empire (1835 to today)
14. Flanders Fields (1914 to 1951)
15. Language and the federal state (1830 to today)

**Culture**

16. Flemish Primitives to Bruegel
17. Rembrandt, Vermeer, Rubens
18. Modern art
19. Architecture
20. Languages
21. Food and drink
22. Feasts and traditions
23. Sport
24. Comics and pop culture
25. Thinkers and scientists
26. How the countries work today

Every unit is built around a year-slider map of its own, and at least 70% of its questions are answered on a map or a picture. Question types include Who am I?, true or myth, fill-the-gap on primary sources, cause and effect, odd one out, sorting, find-the-year, detail hunts in paintings and before-and-after swipes. Lessons on slavery, conquest, the Holocaust and other hard subjects open with a content note and run in a calm mode.

## Around the units (PRs #29 to #40)

- **Placement test** (#29): Unit 0. Answer five questions a unit, and four right opens the whole unit.
- **Crowns and Legendary** (#30): five levels per lesson. Level 5 is a timed 15-question challenge across the unit.
- **Mastery gate** (#31): a unit opens once the checkpoint before it is passed with 8 of 10 right. History forks after Unit 9, and culture is open from the start.
- **Museum wall** (#32): 507 cards of the artworks, people, buildings and food the lessons show. A card is hung as its lesson is finished, in one room per unit.
- **Daily quests** (#33): three goals a day, each worth 10 XP.
- **Progress export and import** (#34): save progress to a file and load it on another device. Nothing is replaced until you confirm.
- **Stork mascot** (#35): a different hat for each unit. It cheers you on, and it stays out of lessons with a content note.
- **Then and now** (#36): ten places shown in an old picture and today, with a divider to drag between them.
- **City explorer** (#37): seven old town centres street by street from OpenStreetMap, with 56 landmarks to read about or find.
- **Museum walks** (#38): the Groeninge, the Mauritshuis, the Rijksmuseum and the KMSKA, walked one artwork at a time.
- **Map stories** (#39): each unit retold in about 90 seconds on its map, read aloud, followed by three find-the-year questions.
- **Dutch and French interface** (#40): the app's own words in English, Dutch or French. The lessons stay in English.

Also included:
- A spaced daily review
- XP and a day streak with one free missed day a week
- A time map of 47 snapshots from Caesar to today
- A province map drill
- A phrasebook of 100 phrases, 28 of them recorded by speakers
